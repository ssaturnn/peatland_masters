Agreement, so the FIRST email goes to **Library@atu.ie** (short version
below) — they hold the institutional channel and a Map Data Request
Form (atlantictu.libguides.com/maps/requests). Only if the 25 cm ortho
GeoTIFFs are outside the standard academic bundle, send the full letter
to **customer.services@tailte.ie**.

Agreement — MSc research request

## Full version — to customer.services@tailte.ie (fallback)
**Subject:** Academic research access to national orthophotography —
MSc project on protected peatland monitoring (ATU Galway)

---

Dear Tailte Éireann team,

I am an MSc Computing student at Atlantic Technological University,
Galway (supervisor: Dr. Brian McGinley), working on a research project
that monitors industrial turf-cutting inside legally-protected Irish
peatlands (SAC and NHA bogs in Counties Galway, Mayo and Roscommon)
using open Sentinel-2 satellite imagery and unsupervised machine
learning.

To evaluate the accuracy of our satellite-based detections we need a
high-resolution reference source. Tailte Éireann's national 25 cm
orthophotography would be ideal: our protocol requires visual labelling
of approximately 300 sample points (point-in-imagery interpretation
only — no bulk redistribution of imagery) across six protected bogs,
plus qualitative verification of detected cutting areas.

Could you advise:

1. whether an academic/research licence is available for the national
   orthophotography (viewing via MapGenie or equivalent WMS access
   would fully cover our needs);
2. whether Atlantic Technological University already holds an
   institutional licence we could use;
3. the appropriate route and any costs for a single-student research
   use of this kind.

The project's outputs are non-commercial: an MSc thesis and an open,
reproducible monitoring methodology. We would of course acknowledge
Tailte Éireann as the reference-imagery source in all outputs.

Thank you for your time,

Aleksandr Turchaninov
MSc Computing, Atlantic Technological University, Galway
Supervisor: Dr. Brian McGinley
